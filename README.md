# Antics

Antics is a pass-the-phone party game app. One phone goes round the room,
whoever is holding it reads the card out, and everyone plays in person.

## The nine games

- Antics mode: a bit of everything, one card at a time
- Never Have I Ever
- Truth or Dare
- Most Likely To
- Would You Rather
- What Are The Odds
- Imposter: everyone sees the secret word except the imposter, who bluffs
- The Four Kings: a classic circle card game where every rank has its own rule
- Spicy: an 18+ mode with Premium

Free to download, with Premium for the full decks. Eleven languages, no ads,
no sign-up, and it works offline once installed.

## Get it

- iPhone (rated 18+ on the App Store) and Android: [anticsapp.com](https://anticsapp.com/)

## Public repositories

- [party-data](https://github.com/GMAntics/party-data): anonymous aggregates from
  parties played on Antics, 26 August to 4 October 2026. When parties start, the
  busiest night, games per party, the first game, truth versus dare, and card
  skip rates and seconds on screen by game. Shares, ratios and medians only.
  Free to use under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):
  please credit Antics and link to [anticsapp.com/party-data](https://anticsapp.com/party-data/).
- [antics-site](https://github.com/GMAntics/antics-site): the source of anticsapp.com,
  with rules guides, question lists and the free browser games. Public to read,
  but the site code is not openly licensed; only its party data is CC BY 4.0.
